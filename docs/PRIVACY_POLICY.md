# World Cup 2026 — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is collected and processed by **Cinexis** ("**us**," "**we**," or "**our**") when you use the **World Cup 2026** mobile application (the "**App**"), published on Google Play (package: `com.techmomentum.wc2026`).

This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the **GDPR** (EU/EEA/UK) and **COPPA** (US), where relevant.

We update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date and, where required, provide additional notice or request renewed consent. Please review this policy whenever you use the App.

---

## About World Cup 2026

**World Cup 2026** is a digital sticker album game. The App **requires sign-in** and stores gameplay progress on our servers so your collection syncs across sessions.

**We want to be clear about what we collect:**

| Data | Collected? | Where stored |
|------|------------|--------------|
| Email and account ID | **Yes** (sign-in required) | Firebase (Google Cloud) |
| Profile (username, name, country, photo) | **Yes** (you provide) | Firebase |
| Sticker collection, packs, slot progress | **Yes** | Firebase |
| Leaderboard stats (username, country, counts) | **Yes** (visible to signed-in users) | Firebase |
| Health or medical data | **No** | — |
| Precise GPS location | **No** | — |

We **do not sell** your personal data.

## 1. Collection of Information

### A. Information you provide directly

When you create an account or complete your profile, we may collect:

- **Email address** (email/password registration or Google Sign-In)
- **Authentication identifiers** (Firebase user ID)
- **Username**, first name, and last name
- **Country** (for profile and leaderboard grouping)
- **Profile photo** (optional, if you upload one)

### B. Information collected automatically through the App

We and our service providers may automatically collect:

- **Device information** (device model, operating system version, app version)
- **IP address** (processed by hosting, analytics, and ad partners)
- **App usage and performance data** (screens used, session activity, crash reports)
- **Firebase Analytics** data
- **Advertising ID** (e.g. Google Advertising ID), where permitted by law and device settings
- **FCM push token** (if you enable notifications)

### C. Game and activity data

To operate gameplay, we store:

- Owned stickers and duplicate counts
- Pack inventory and opening history
- Album completion statistics
- Slot machine spins, rewards, and cooldown timestamps
- Ad reward and daily reward claim timestamps
- In-app settings (sound, haptics, notifications)

### D. Information we receive through other channels

If you contact us by **email** or through a **website contact form**, we receive whatever information you choose to send (support requests, deletion requests, feedback). We use that information only to respond and resolve your request.

## 2. Processing and Usage of Information

### Personal information we use to:

- Create and manage your account
- Provide core App features (album, packs, slot machine, swap deck, leaderboard)
- Sync your progress across sessions and devices
- Validate rewards and prevent abuse (server-side)
- Send optional notifications you enable
- Respond to support and data-rights requests

### Advertising ID and non-personal information we use to:

- Serve **contextual** and, where permitted, **personalized** advertisements via **Google AdMob**
- Measure ad delivery and performance
- Analyze installs, sessions, countries, devices, and general usage trends
- Maintain, debug, and improve the App

### Ad formats in this App

- **Banner ads**
- **Interstitial ads** (e.g. after certain actions such as opening packs)
- **Rewarded video ads** (optional — watch to earn in-game rewards such as stickers or slot spins)

## 3. Storage of Information

We store your data for as long as necessary to:

- Provide the App and your account
- Maintain backups and security
- Comply with legal, tax, or regulatory obligations

We **do not sell** personal information to third parties.
